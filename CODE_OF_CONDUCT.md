# Code of Conduct

Super Converter follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be welcoming and respectful. Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Keep discussion about the work: the file, the conversion, the design, the code.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind; take feedback as information, not as an attack.
- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and unwelcome sexual attention are not tolerated in
  issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Scope

This applies to every project space (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, the website) and to
anyone representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact on their GitHub profile
([@udsy19](https://github.com/udsy19)). Reports are handled confidentially; you'll hear back within a few days.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove or edit contributions that don't follow this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is inappropriate, threatening or harmful, following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
