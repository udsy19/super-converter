- **Name:** Super Converter
- **Bundle ID:** com.udsy.superconverter.appstore (+ six `.quickactions.*` extension ids)
- **SKU:** superconverter-appstore
- **Category:** Utilities (Info.plist `LSApplicationCategoryType`)
- **Price:** Free (keeps the unRAR licence condition satisfied — see known-blockers 10)
- **Minimum macOS:** 14.0
- **Subtitle:** Convert files right on your Mac
- **Promotional text:** Drop a file, pick a format, done. Images, PDFs, documents, audio, video, archives and fonts — converted on your Mac, never uploaded.
- **Description:**
  Super Converter turns files into the format you need without sending them anywhere.
  • Images: HEIC, RAW (DNG and camera RAW), PSD/PSB, XCF, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, PNG, JPG, icons, and tracing to SVG
  • Documents: Word, OpenDocument, RTF, Markdown (with maths), HTML, plain text, and PDF to editable Word or text
  • Spreadsheets: Excel, CSV, OpenDocument
  • Vector and CAD: SVG, PDF, EMF/WMF, Illustrator, XPS, DXF
  • E-books: EPUB, comic archives (CBZ) to PDF
  • Audio and video: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, MP3, FLAC, Ogg, WMA, GIF and more
  • Archives: open ZIP, 7Z, RAR, TAR, ISO, DMG, CAB, LHA and more; create ZIP, 7Z and TAR, with passwords
  • Fonts: TTF, OTF, WOFF, WOFF2
  Batch convert from the window or the menu bar, or right-click files in Finder and use Quick Actions.
  Unsafe archives (path tricks, symlinks, decompression bombs) are caught before anything is written.
